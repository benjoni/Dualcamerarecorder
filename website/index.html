Record

Built around the REC button

Grey with a red edge while you wait. Solid red STOP while you roll. Everything else on the screen is there to tell you the take is safe.

  • Rehearsal records both pictures without rolling the cameras.
  • IN / OUT while rolling, so playback starts where the action does.
  • Card and camera clip numbers per camera, saved with the slate.
  • Cameras A and B out of the box. Add more cameras in Project settings and pick which one sits on each input.
  • Captured, written and dropped frames counted live, with free disk time.
Camera settings

Lens, stop and distance with every take

Press Camera Settings and the fields open under both pictures. Type a bare number and the unit is added: 32 becomes 32mm, 2.8 becomes T2.8, 5600 becomes 5600K.

  • Lens, Stop, Distance, Height, Tilt, Filter, ISO, Shutter, WB for camera A and B.
  • Values carry over to the next take until you change them, and lock while rolling.
  • Saved in the clip metadata, the clip log and the PDF report, and shown in Playback.
Rigging

Camera upside down? Flip it.

Flip and Flop turn the picture on screen, on the monitor, on the phones and in the recorded file. Frame lines for 1.85, 2:1, 2.35 or your own ratio sit on top.

LUT

See the look. Record the original.

Import your .cube files and pick one for each camera. The live picture shows the look, and the file stays exactly as the camera sends it.

  • Several LUTs at once: 3D or 1D .cube files from DaVinci Resolve or the camera maker.
  • One per camera, saved with the project.
  • On the live picture: screen, waveform, Blackmagic monitor and phones.
  • Playback and Edit show the recorded material, without the LUT.
Overlay

Freeze a frame from any recorded take and lay the live camera over it. Match an actor’s mark or a camera position in seconds.

Edit

A director’s cut on set

Drop the selects on a timeline, keep several sequences per project and export an H.264 film with the sound of either camera. Clip names can be burned into the picture.

Connect a phone

Scan. Watch.

  1. On the Mac, turn on Settings → Live view on phones. A QR code appears.
  2. Point the phone camera at it and open the link. Same Wi-Fi, any browser, no app.
  3. Both cameras play live, with the REC light, timecode and the current slate. The Mac shows how many phones are watching.

The address dualcam.local stays the same when the router hands out a new IP, so a printed QR code keeps working.

Phone slate

Any phone is the clapper

One tap turns the live view into a black slate with scene, shot, take and card numbers.

  1. Hold the phone in front of the lens and tap CLAP, or CLAP A / CLAP B when the cameras are far apart.
  2. Three short beeps count down: 3 · 2 · 1.
  3. The screen flashes white for exactly one frame, with a beep. Sync picture and sound to it in the edit.

In full screen, scene, shot, take and card numbers can be corrected right on the phone.

Safety

Disk pulled out? The take is still there.

Files are written in two-second fragments. If the recording disk disappears, the take stops at once and everything already on the disk plays back.

  • Slow disk warning the moment frames wait to be written.
  • Backup copy to a second disk after every take, verified before it counts.
  • Clean monitor feed to a Blackmagic monitor: camera A, B or both.
Reports

The paperwork writes itself

Everything the script supervisor types into the slate goes into the files, the log and the report.

PDF clip report

One click at wrap. Every shooting day starts a new page: thumbnails from camera A and B, scene, shot and take, timecode in and out, notes and camera settings. Landscape with 6 clips per page or portrait with 8, all takes or only the starred ones.

Waveform on the picture

Press ⇧⌘W and a luma waveform sits over both cameras. Check exposure and match A to B without a separate scope.

Questions

Before the first shoot

Do the cameras need genlock?

No. Both inputs start on hardware timestamps, so takes line up within half a frame without genlock.

Why A and B? We call our second camera C.

The cameras are A and B out of the box. In Project settings you can add more cameras to the list, for example C, and switch which camera is on input 1 and input 2. File names, slate, log and report follow.

Can I record just one camera?

Yes. Switch the project to one camera and camera A fills the screen. A camera can also be switched off for a few takes.

Does the crew need an app on their phones?

No. They scan the QR code and the live view opens in the browser, on any phone or tablet on the same Wi-Fi.

Can the camera start the recording?

Yes. With Record with the camera turned on, the REC flag in the camera’s SDI signal starts and stops the take, and the camera’s timecode is saved with every clip.

What happens when the disk fills up or is pulled out?

You see the remaining recording time all the time. If the disk disappears, the take stops and everything written so far is saved and playable.
